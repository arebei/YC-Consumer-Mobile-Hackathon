A walking game for your neighbourhood

Walk a loop. Own the blocks inside it.

Lasso turns the streets around you into a game against the people who live there. Local shops put up the prizes.

01Claim

Every block inside your loop is yours.

Walk or run a closed loop and finish where you started. The map is split into blocks, and every block inside your loop takes your colour.

A bigger loop makes a stronger claim: each block's strength is the number of blocks your loop enclosed. Laps around the same loop add nothing.

02Steal

Until a neighbour walks a bigger one.

A rival takes your blocks only by enclosing them in a larger loop than the one that claimed them. You hear about it straight away, with the exact size that takes them back.

Sam took 3 of your blocks with a 9-block loop: thanks for the coffee. Enclose 10 to take them back.

03Flags

Plant a flag. Say something.

Every player has one flag: a short message planted on every block they hold. Neighbours read it when they tap your blocks, and it rides along on the alert when you take theirs.

Change it whenever you like. Your blocks show the new one straight away.

04Shops

Hold the café's block. The coffee is free.

Some blocks have a local shop in them. Whoever holds that block for the most hours this week wins the shop's reward, collected in person at the counter.

01How it works

Four steps from a walk to a free coffee.

The map is split into hexagonal blocks about 50 m across. Everyone who has the app plays on the same map.

  1. Step 1

    Walk a loop

    Start the app, walk or run a closed loop, and finish where you started. Only walking and running count.

  2. Step 2

    Claim the blocks

    Every block whose centre is inside your loop takes your colour and your flag. Bigger loops make stronger claims.

  3. Step 3

    Steal with bigger loops

    A neighbour takes your blocks only with a loop of more blocks than yours. You get an alert with the size it takes to win them back.

  4. Step 4

    Win shop rewards

    Some blocks hold a partner shop. Hold that block the longest by the end of the week and its reward is yours.

02Points and wallet

Every walk earns points.

1 pointfor every 10 m you walk

+10 pointsfor every block you claim

A 520 m loop that claims 7 blocks earns 52 + 70 = 122 points.

  1. Exchange your points for offers at shops near you: a coffee, a pastry, money off.

  2. The voucher lands in your in-app wallet.

  3. Show it at the counter and the shop redeems it.

03For shops

Put up a prize. Pay only when someone walks in.

Local businesses put up prizes and offers for the neighbourhood. Walkers come to your door to claim them, and you pay a small fee only for each voucher redeemed at your counter, invoiced through Whop. No listing fee, no monthly plan.

  • New feetPeople walk loops around your block to win your reward.
  • Per redemptionA small fee only when a voucher is used in person.
  • Whop invoicesEach fee arrives as an invoice with a payment link.

06FAQ

Questions

Is Lasso free?

Yes. Players pay nothing. Shops fund the rewards and pay a small fee only when a voucher is redeemed.

Does it track me in the background?

No. Lasso only uses your location while the app is open on your screen. Close it and tracking stops.

How big are blocks?

Blocks are hexagons about 50 m across. A square loop of about 130 m a side, a six-minute walk, claims around 7 blocks.

How do shops pay?

Per redemption. Each time a voucher is used at the counter, the shop gets a Whop invoice for a small fee with a link to pay it.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. Play in the browser on any Android phone, or install Expo Go from Google Play for the full native app.
